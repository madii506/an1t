How An1t works

A coin, a pair and an ant that trades.

01

Launch

Name, ticker, image. Your coin trades against $ANTHROPIC, the Anthropic token on Solana.

02

Give it a mandate

Tell your ant how to trade in plain English, pick a risk level, a trade size and a daily cap.

03

It watches the market

Your ant reads live Solana markets (Jupiter, Dexscreener): momentum, organic volume, liquidity, buyers vs sellers.

04

It trades for you

It picks a move and explains why. On autopilot it opens each trade in your wallet. Swaps run through Jupiter.

The ant's brain

Each think cycle sends your mandate, limits, holdings and the live board to an Anthropic model (Claude Haiku, through Vercel's AI Gateway). It must pick one coin from the board, or wait. It can't invent coins, and it can't move more than your trade size. If the model is unreachable, plain rules take over and the console says so.

  • Your keys stay yoursEvery trade is a Jupiter swap your wallet signs. An1t never holds your funds.
  • Limits you setPer-trade size, trades per day, and take-profit / stop levels by risk style.
  • Everything in the openThe console shows every scan, thought and move with the numbers behind it.

FAQ

Does An1t hold my money?

No. Every trade is a Jupiter swap built for your wallet and signed by you. An1t never sees your keys.

What does autopilot do?

With autopilot on, your ant opens each trade in your wallet as soon as it decides, within your trade size and daily cap. You confirm in the wallet.

What is $ANTHROPIC?

The Anthropic PreStocks token on Solana, a third-party pre-IPO token. Coins launched here trade against it. It is not an Anthropic share and An1t is not affiliated with Anthropic.

What can I tell my ant?

Anything in plain English: what to buy, what to avoid, when to take profit. Type questions in the console and it answers with live data.

Can I run more than one ant?

Yes. Add up to five ants to your colony, each with its own mandate and risk. Awake ants take turns reading the market.

What does the scanner check?

Mint and freeze authority, top 10 holder share, liquidity against market cap, buys vs sells, organic score and age, then your ant gives a buy, watch or avoid verdict.
